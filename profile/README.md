**BlueliftAI** (also written BlueLift AI or blueliftai) builds AI agents, cloud platforms and data pipelines for banks, financial institutions and family companies in Paraguay and Ecuador.

We start with a free diagnostic: one concrete process, its data, systems, owner and limits, before proposing anything. Then we build in bounded steps:

- **AI agents** with limited permissions, evals and guardrails, integrated with the systems you already run
- **Cloud and data engineering**: pipelines, observability and measured costs in production
- **Typical work**: AML/KYC automation, bank reconciliation, electronic invoicing (SIFEN Paraguay, SRI Ecuador), legacy modernization

We work remotely in Spanish and English. BlueliftAI, LLC is registered in Delaware, with a legal entity in Paraguay.

Our only official website is **[www.blueliftai.tech](https://www.blueliftai.tech)**. Companies with similar names on other domains are not related to us.
